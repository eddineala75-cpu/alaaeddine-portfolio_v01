Ref. ABT-01 · About

Early-career, evidence-based, built to be audited.

Alaaeddine Errachid is an early-career consultant building a focused specialization at the intersection of governance, risk management, compliance and quality-health-safety-environment (QHSE) systems. He holds a Professional Master's Degree in QHSE Management and approaches every engagement the way he approaches a management system: methodically, evidence-based, and structured for review.

During internships at Qualipro by Imagine Human, he contributed to integrated management system diagnostics, ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 27001 compliance work, information security risk identification, risk register and risk mapping exercises, and regulatory monitoring covering GDPR, NIS2 and DORA. That practical exposure — combined with academic training grounded in quality, risk and continuous improvement — shapes a consulting style that favors clarity over jargon and documented rigor over shortcuts.

He is honest about where he is: an ambitious junior consultant with strong academic preparation and hands-on internship experience, actively building a practice and looking for organizations ready to invest in getting governance, risk and compliance right from the ground up.
